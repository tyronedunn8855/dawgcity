04Our story

Founded by Ken and Latonya Hughes, built on the flavors of the cities they love.

Dawg City started in 2021 with one idea: take a 100% all-beef hotdog in its natural casing, then top it with quality sausage, brats, and fresh local ingredients until it tastes like nowhere else in Milwaukee.

Chicago sport peppers, Philly shaved steak, Southern pulled pork and slaw. Grab a seat at the dine-in bar on the first floor of 3rd St. Market Hall, or take your food over to the game.
