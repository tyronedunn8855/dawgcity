Our Story

Founded in 2021 by Ken and Latonya Hughes, Dawg City brings East Coast and Southern flavor to downtown Milwaukee.

Every dawg starts with a 100% all-beef hotdog in its natural casing, topped with quality sausage, brats, and fresh local produce. Chicago sport peppers, Philly shaved steak, Southern pulled pork and slaw — one basket at a time.

Find us on the first floor of 3rd St. Market Hall. Grab a seat at the dine-in bar or carry your basket over to your game.
